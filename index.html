Common Questions

Simple answers before you apply.

How soon should I get pre-approved?

As early as possible. Pre-qualification helps define budget and strengthens your position when you make an offer.

What is needed to pre-qualify?

Typical documents include two years of tax returns and W-2s, recent paystubs, recent bank statements, and a photo ID.

Broker vs. direct lender?

A direct lender offers its own products. A broker can shop multiple lenders to find a better-fit program.
